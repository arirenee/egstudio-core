### 3 — How a sprint runs
**H2:** Pay. Brief. Pilot. Three batches.

**Steps:**
1. **Pay** — $1,500 USD on Stripe. One sprint. One seat.
2. **Brief** — After payment, submit the questionnaire: footage links, fonts, colors, brand rules. No discovery call.

## 3. FAQ answers (rewritten)

**How does white-label handoff work?**  
Deliverables go to an unbranded Frame.io link or a named Drive folder. We do not contact your clients. Files carry no EG Studio metadata.

**What footage can we send?**  
Up to 90 minutes of source per sprint (Zoom, podcast, iPhone, keynote, product capture). Work beyond 90 minutes is scoped separately through ops@egstudio.tech.

**When do videos land?**  
15 finished videos in 30 days, in three batches on Days 10, 20, and 30. Each asset includes two revision rounds. After timestamped notes land in the review portal, the revision turn is 2 business days. The full set is not a 2-day job.

**What is the pilot?**  
Days 1–3: one pilot to lock type, color, pacing, and captions. The rest of the batch starts after written sign-off.

**Can we set our own resale price?**  
Yes. Your price to your client is yours. Wholesale cost is $1,500 per sprint. Any retainer math on this page is a model, not a promised outcome.

**Is this a monthly contract?**  
No. Buy sprints as you close work. Repeat sprints when a seat is open.

**What if intake is closed?**  
Checkout is hidden and the hero says intake is closed. Email ops@egstudio.tech for the next queue. Do not pay if the page says intake is closed.

**Who is this for?**  
5–20 person B2B marketing agencies and content leads who already sell (or want to sell) vertical video retainers and need finished 9:16 without hiring an editor bench.
